Everything an article can include

A one-page view of what exists. The numbered sections below cover each field in detail, in the order they appear in the form.

Headline area

  • Headline
  • Summary
  • Eyebrow label above the headline
  • Publish date
  • Breadcrumb trail
  • Author byline

Banner

  • Banner image
  • Brightcove video or Captivate podcast
  • Custom title above the play button
  • Running time
  • "Listen on" links: Apple, Spotify, Google

Navigation

  • Optional series menu bar
  • Presets: Biopharma, Healthcare, Strategic Alternatives, Real-Time Geopolitics, Partner of Choice
  • Or up to 4 custom links

Body

  • Rich-text article, or an external link instead
  • Key takeaways box (3 to 5 points)
  • Up to 4 extra podcast players
  • Footnotes with a custom heading
  • Transcript link

Right side

  • Authors ("Our experts")
  • Featured guests
  • Callout / promo box
  • Up to 3 related stories

People

  • Up to 4 authors from the author library
  • Up to 6 featured guests
  • Each with name, title, photo, email, LinkedIn, Twitter, bio

Tiles & listings

  • Tile image and summary
  • Story type icon: text, video or podcast
  • Read / listen time
  • Play episode button (podcasts)
  • Optional transcript link

Where it shows

  • 26 listing locations
  • Insights, the podcast pages, AI, Geopolitics, Energy Transition and more

Search & settings

  • SEO description and keywords
  • Tags
  • Canonical URL
  • Article type, language, region

Quick start: the minimum for a new story

Everything else is optional, filled in automatically, or only used when requested.

  1. Publish DateThe go-live date, as YYYY-MM-DD
  2. Headline TitleUnder 70 characters
  3. SummaryAbout 120 characters (140 max), plain text
  4. Tile / Thumbnail Image1200 × 628 .webp, under 300 KB
  5. Banner Image1920 × 475 .webp, under 300 KB
  6. Breadcrumb LinkThe section the story belongs to
  7. Story TypeText, Video or Podcast
  8. ContentThe article body, or an External URL
  9. Author(s)Picked from the author library
  10. Location to FeatureWhere it should appear, only when ready to go live
  11. SEO DescriptionFrom the business, or reuse the Summary

Filled in automatically when you save

No need to type these. Leave them blank, save, then check the result.

FieldHow it is filled
Publish DateToday's date, if empty when the form opens
Subcategory Tiles"Insights"
Preview Breadcrumb TextFrom the breadcrumb you picked, e.g. Home | Insights | Podcasts | Macro Minutes
Preview Author Display TextFrom the authors: "Jane Doe and John Smith", or "A, B & C" for three or more
Time to readText Only stories: word count divided by 250, e.g. "6 min"
Canonical URLAn internal address; the live page always uses its clean public address
SEO KeywordsCopied from the breadcrumb if empty (replace with real keywords)
TagsCopied from the breadcrumb if empty
Page addressYear and month from the Publish Date plus the headline, e.g. 2026/10/canadian-equity-compass. Set when the story is first saved.

1Basics

  • Type of Article
    Required

    Insights Article for a normal story. Related Content Article/Link saves it to the related-content folder instead of a dated folder.

    Insights ArticleRelated Content Article/Link
  • Publish Date
    Required

    Shown on the story and its tiles, and used to sort stories newest first.

    The form's own note: save with today's date, then change to 0000-00-00 before publishing for preview.

    YYYY-MM-DD
  • Headline Title
    Required

    The story's title. Keep it short for search results. Dollar signs are handled automatically.

    Under 70 charactersBox allows 100
  • Summary
    Recommended

    Short description under the title and on tiles. No HTML and no line breaks.

    About 120 characters140 maxPlain text
  • Above Title Eyebrow
    If requested

    Small label above the headline.

  • Subcategory Tiles
    Auto

    Label used on tiles. Defaults to "Insights"; leave it unless asked.

2Navigation & breadcrumbs

  • Secondary Navigation
    If requested

    A menu bar under the site menu, for campaign or series pages.

    No NavigationCustomize NavigationBiopharmaHealthcareStrategic AlternativesReal-Time GeopoliticsPartner of Choice
  • Customize Secondary Navigation
    If requested

    With "Customize Navigation" selected, add your own menu items, each with a Menu Name and a Menu Link.

    Up to 4 items
  • Breadcrumb Link
    Recommended

    The trail at the top of the page. Pick the section the story belongs to; defaults to Insights. It also fills in the Tags and SEO Keywords if those are blank. All options are listed below.

  • Manual breadcrumb
    If requested

    For a trail that isn't in the list. Entered as HTML by the web team. Overrides the dropdown.

  • Preview Breadcrumb Text
    Auto

    Read-only preview of the trail, built on save.

4Video & podcast

  • Video Link from Brightcove
    Optional

    The video in the banner. For RBCCM videos, use the Brightcove player link from the RBCCM account, starting //players.brightcove.net (no https:). For an outside video, use the full https:// link.

  • Audio / Podcast Link from Captivate
    Optional

    The full Captivate player link for the episode, e.g. https://player.captivate.fm/episode/.... This is what plays when someone presses Play on the story's tile.

    If both a video and a podcast are filled in, the video shows in the banner.

  • Custom Banner Video / Podcast Title
    Optional

    Title shown above the video or listen button in the banner.

  • Show that title after the Article Title
    Optional

    Yes moves the title above to sit after the headline and before the summary.

    YesNo
  • Banner Audio links
    Optional

    The "Listen on" links in the banner. Pick a show to use its standard links.

    No LinksCustom LinksBiopharmaIndustries in MotionMarkets in MotionStrategic AlternativesPowering Sustainable IdeasMacro Minutes
  • Custom Banner Audio Links
    Optional

    Your own links. These override the show's standard links.

    AppleSpotifyGoogle
  • Podcast Player within Content
    Optional

    Extra Captivate players placed inside the article body. Ask the web team how to position them.

    Up to 4 players
  • Podcast or Video Transcript Link
    Optional

    Link to the transcript page (shortened URL).

  • Display Transcript Link at Bottom
    Optional

    Hide only if the player is in the Content section and the link would appear twice.

    ShowHide
  • Podcast Tile Transcript Link
    Optional

    What the podcast's tile shows next to the icon.

    Show X minShow Transcript Link
  • Time to Watch or Listen
    Optional

    Running time shown in the banner.

    e.g. 23 min

5Story content

  • Content or External URL
    Required

    Choose one. Content is the article body, written in the rich-text editor. External URL sends the story's tile to another page instead of a story page.

  • Keypoints
    Optional

    The key takeaways box. Each point ends with a period.

    3 to 5 points215 characters each
  • Custom Footnotes heading
    Optional

    Defaults to "+ Footnotes".

  • Footnotes
    Optional

    Footnotes below the article, as a bulleted list.

6Story type & timing

  • Story Type
    Required

    Controls the icon shown with the time on tiles. Podcast stories also get the Play episode button on their tiles.

    Text OnlyVideo / WatchPodcast / Audio / Listen
  • Time to read
    Auto for text

    Whole number, rounded down. For Text Only stories, leave it blank and it fills in on save. For video and podcast stories, enter it yourself.

    Format: X min

12Location to Feature

Tick each page the story should be listed on. Listing pages and story tiles, such as the podcast pages, pull stories by these ticks, newest Publish Date first.

Only tick these when the story is ready to go live. Ticking a location on an unfinished story puts it on the live page. Check those pages after it publishes.

13SEO & tags

  • SEO Description
    Required

    Shown in search results. Provided by the business; if there isn't one, reuse the Summary.

    160 characters max
  • SEO Keywords
    RequiredAuto if empty

    Separated by commas. If left blank it copies the breadcrumb (e.g. "Home, Insights, Podcasts"), so add real keywords after saving.

    6 to 9 keywords
  • Tags
    RequiredAuto if empty

    If left blank it copies the breadcrumb. Replace or add to them after saving if needed.

    Auto

    Leave empty.
