Frances Donald | Aug 12, 2026

BoC Rate Decision Preview

Inflation tick-up reduces probability of a 50bps cut next week; 25bps remains baseline.

Headline CPI came in two tenths above consensus, and the trimmed-mean measures the Bank watches most closely stopped decelerating for the first time since March. That is not, on its own, an argument against easing.

Financial conditions have loosened materially since the spring. Our baseline: 25bps next week, 25bps in the following meeting, and a terminal rate of 2.25% by mid-2026.

Iran conflict: six-month update

Diplomatic resolution remains elusive; markets modestly pricing in a sustained Strait of Hormuz premium.

As the Iran conflict reaches its six-month mark, diplomatic resolution remains elusive despite repeated market optimism about swift resolution. Energy markets have partially normalized but structural risk persists.

Our base case: modest crude premium through year-end, with tail risk on any escalation into shipping lanes. Strategic petroleum reserves remain the swing factor.
