Meet the host

Valerie Grimba

Director, Global ETF Strategy, RBC Capital Markets

Valerie runs the Global ETF Strategy team at RBC Capital Markets, helping institutional clients understand and implement ETF strategies and bridging the gap between clients and ETF trade execution. She sits at the center of a desk that serves as designated broker to roughly 300 ETF mandates and acts as authorized participant across more than 90% of the Canadian ETF market — working with everyone from the largest pension funds to family offices.

With over 15 years of global institutional sales and trading experience, Valerie’s career has spanned trading floors across Canada, the U.S. and Australasia, including roles at Bear Stearns, CIBC World Markets and Macquarie before joining RBC.
